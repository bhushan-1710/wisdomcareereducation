## 6. Tone guide
- Short sentences. Plain words. Warm but professional.
- Speak to the student first, with the parent in mind ("you", "your").
- Specific over vague: "Free counselling in Nashik or online" instead of "buzz us" (only if online counselling is confirmed [TBC]).
- No hype, no countdowns, no fake urgency, no guaranteed outcomes.
